# OUTCOME-MARGIN-001 — robust counterfactual backtrack labels (17 November 2026)

**Result: negative. No controller-independent, eight-cell backtrack advantage was found in either TRAIN or DEVELOPMENT.** Do not train another recovery adapter on these labels yet.

## Hypothesis and method

The predecessor [OUTCOME-FORK-001](https://github.com/techrote/DRIV3R/blob/a278bcfe6d86aa828477c0038816c06d22a4b815/experiments/driv3r/training/long_horizon/OUTCOME_FORK_RESULT_2026-11-17.md) labelled immediate MOVE versus BACKTRACK using 128-step continuation from one frozen policy. This audit varies the *suffix controller*: frozen, forward imitation, geometry contrastive, and outcome-supervised. It restores **eight SHA-256-verified checkpoints** across seeds 811 and 814 and executes both initial actions using the same subsequent policy on otherwise identical synthetic worlds.

TRAIN and DEVELOPMENT are evaluated separately: four terrain families × two spur types × one orientation × two seeds × four controllers = **64 paired comparisons per split**, 128 paired comparisons, 256 full first-action counterfactual continuations in total. Each branch is 128 decisions at most. Full per-action distributions are computed at the initial choice; action histograms, STOP counts, latency, original checkpoint hashes, and each cell-painting difference are in the ledger. There is no optimizer or new checkpoint.

A *robust substantial backtrack win* requires BACKTRACK to paint **at least eight more new cells in all eight seed/controller combinations** for a matched world.

## Results

| Metric | TRAIN | DEVELOPMENT |
|---|---:|---:|
| Distinct terrain / spur cases | 8 | 8 |
| Paired counterfactuals | 64 | 64 |
| Cases where any suffix favoured BACKTRACK | 4/8 | 4/8 |
| Cases with contradictory MOVE/BACKTRACK winning signs | 4/8 | 4/8 |
| Robust BACKTRACK advantage of 8+ cells | **0/8** | **0/8** |

| Suffix controller | TRAIN: backtrack wins / 16 | DEVELOPMENT: backtrack wins / 16 | DEV mean (MOVE − BACKTRACK) new cells |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen | 4 | 3 | +2.812 |
| Forward imitation | 3 | 3 | +1.688 |
| Geometry contrastive | 6 | 7 | +0.562 |
| Outcome-supervised | 3 | 1 | +3.688 |

Particularly revealing DEVELOPMENT examples: at the *same* pickets blocked-spur fork, contrastive continuation favoured BACKTRACK by 37 or 34 cells depending on seed, but frozen or outcome-supervised continuation instead favoured MOVE or tied. In the zigzag blocked-spur case, one contrastive seed favoured BACKTRACK by 34 cells, while the corresponding outcome-supervised suffix favoured MOVE by one cell. The label is therefore a *policy-contingent outcome*, not an intrinsic property of the corridor geometry.

The compact [evidence ledger](OUTCOME_MARGIN_EVIDENCE_2026-11-17.json) contains the 16 exact case matrices (8 comparisons each), action-distribution totals, STOP counts, mean measured neural selection latency, eight checkpoint SHA-256 identities and source/archive hashes.

## Boundaries and next experiment

**Scope:** synthetic 96×96 material worlds only; no native CyberSand receipt, no verified physical 128-step native authority, no structural HELDOUT inspection, and no paid computation. The eight suffix controllers are related, not independent model families. Only orientation zero was examined. This is an intentionally bounded negative result, not a universal impossibility theorem.

Next: search or construct TRAIN-only forks with **substantial, policy-stable** BACKTRACK gains, keep legal menus and recovery budgets matched, require a margin across several frozen suffix controllers before training labels, then evaluate the rule once on independent DEVELOPMENT and eventually sealed structural HELDOUT. No automatic new Actions workflow is warranted.
