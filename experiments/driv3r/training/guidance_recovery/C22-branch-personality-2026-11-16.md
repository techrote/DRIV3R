# C22 — Branch-rich personality preservation under active host advice

**16 November 2026 · DRIV3R guidance/recovery · RESEARCH ONLY / NEGATIVE PROMOTION**

## Scientific question

Can a bounded, trainable residual on personality-preferred **legal INK** actions restore the painting character that host-attested recovery (`C18-A0`) suppresses, without degrading valid recovery or trusting misleading cues? Previous C20/C21 experiments used cramped corridors and rarely observed two-sided turn opportunities. Here an external synthetic host uses one-ring open side pockets during training and two-ring, independently slitted and transformed hook/zigzag topologies for held-out evaluation. This does **not** establish CyberSand physical integration or native ABI compatibility.

## Method and fixed boundaries

- Frozen C12 and the untrained C18-A0 host-attested BACKTRACK control, with the seven exact prerequisite checkpoints preserved outside Git.
- Train two 32→12→1 sidecar MLPs using AdamW: C22-BOUNDED (±0.6 legal favourite-INK logit; strong trust anchor) and C22-STRONG (±1.2; weaker trust anchor). Each uses **912 real optimizer updates** from **2,395 on-policy synthetic-host observed, active-guidance two-sided action menus**.
- Reward-neutral target: recover at most 0.045 absolute personality-favoured INK probability mass toward frozen C12, with separate policy-distribution drift regularization. **This is not reinforcement learning on episode returns**, and must not be represented as such.
- The host recomputes legality, owns protected cells and actual movement, applies stochastic action samples only after a legal mask, and never implements material Undo. No game-side writes, provider overrides, or hidden-topology policy features.
- Matched arms: no advice, weak 0.35 and strong 1.0 signed host advice; independent 0/0.35/1.0 soft hint arms; two personalities; real source-matched, protected-material execution. Train families: hairpin/staircase. Withheld geometry: expanded hook/zigzag.

## Results — independent held-out trajectories

**2,880 stochastic episodes** over four models; **576 advice-present episodes per model** plus 144 no-cue per model. Full evaluation horizon 56 decisions.

| Model | Goals / 576 guided | Active favourite-INK / two-sided menus | Fixed-state favourite-INK mass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen C12 | 502 | 760 / 2,824 (26.91%) | 26.7879% |
| C18-A0 | 507 | 714 / 2,841 (25.13%) | 25.9100% |
| C22-BOUNDED | 510 | 717 / 2,841 (25.24%) | 26.0350% |
| C22-STRONG | 508 | 738 / 2,838 (26.00%) | 26.3277% |

The fixed-state probabilities are computed on the **same 1,459 host-observed, two-sided menus** independent of candidate rollout divergence. Thus C22-STRONG recovers ~0.42 percentage points relative to C18-A0, but remains ~0.46 pp below C12. It does not establish stable personal style on long paths.

Complete-episode goal success is essentially flat: bounded +3/576, strong +1/576 versus C18-A0. The richer branch environment is valuable evidence of observable turn opportunity, but the new optimizer does not deliver a compelling recovery improvement. Training-loss reduction alone cannot justify promotion.

All **144 no-guidance matched trajectories per new head** preserve exact action-sequence digests and final ink digests versus C12. **0 host-rejected actions, 0 illegal raw top-choice actions** in the held-out set. Protected material SHA-256 is checked after every synthetic host action; ink never disappears. These are synthetic contract checks, not CyberSand native execution.

## Scientific limits and disposition

This is a controlled synthetic simulator whose policy observes historical candidate features, not the canonical CyberSand observation/action contract. Feature index 21 is fixture-specific. The closed set of two-sided choices can still be dominated by goal geometry. Opposite-personality behavior on divergent executed paths can be confounded with success/stop times. The current setup also optimizes probability mass and not actual host-executed long-term return; raw preference selection rates are not equivalent to personality invariance.

**Retain C12 as the preferred trained policy and C18-A0 as the stronger untrained reference. Do not promote either C22 adapter.** C22 shows that meaningful opportunity-rich matched trials are feasible. Next candidate should use joint *return-constrained, opportunity-conditioned* optimization with held-out host-executed validation and non-regression gates for misleading feedback.
