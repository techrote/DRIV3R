## Experimental intervention and exact local evidence identity

C64's aligned and shuffled-credit actors took unequal numbers of training actions. C65 controls this by collecting **68 shared frozen-C60-behaviour episodes containing exactly 4,184 executed legal host actions** on 34 eligible training states. Its ALIGNED and temporally SHIFTED reward-credit arms train offline on *the same action menus, observed features, selected actions, outcomes and minibatch indices*, using **48 CPU AdamW updates each** and clipped importance ratios. SHIFT rotates reward events by half the same episode, preserving its reward multiset and eventual success outcome but changing its discounted returns. This is **offline importance-weighted learning**, not on-policy reinforcement learning.

The independent online evaluation selected a fixed two-graph-per-family subset from six families *before inspecting outcomes*: braid, prim, prim_braid, tree, Wilson and Kruskal; **12 test graph identities**, separated from training and recorded C60–C64 historical graph identities. Test capacity and confidence are limited by this small selection. Two guidance strengths and 0%/50% erased historical menus were evaluated against A0, RESET, non-neural FRONTIER and frozen C60 controls.
