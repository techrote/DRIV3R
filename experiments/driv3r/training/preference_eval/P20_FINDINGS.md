Of the 72 P19 model-derived four-niche visual judgments over these 18 pairs, 57 were A/B votes and 15 ties. **53/57 (92.98%) non-tie judgments preferred the longer continuation.** Aggregating per original world/pair: 15 paired worlds had a majority of non-tie votes favouring the longer trajectory, 2 favoured shorter and 1 had only ties. The four niche votes on the same world are correlated. These judgments came from a GPT-6 assistant pilot whose earlier presentation exposed source cohort; **zero owner/human votes** exist. They are not a statistical validation of human taste, nor proof that longer is creatively better.

The remaining **10 nonidentical historical matched original comparisons** occupy only **three independent source-world blocks**. These genuine ties are useful attention checks, not style winners.

## Scientific decision

P19's 30/94 learned-ranker result and its 73/94 coverage-baseline result remain recorded negatives/controls; P20 reveals an additional *length/termination confound* in the W16 test cohort. A source-disjoint train/test split does not rescue a cohort consisting mostly of same-policy STOP prefixes. **Reject P19 as a basis for aesthetic reward promotion or checkpoint ranking.** Do not retrain a surrogate on these pseudo-labels, create another scalar safety heuristic or select the longest trace as a universal creative optimum.

Strictly retain four niches separately: **Meanderer**, **Curler**, **Brancher** and **Chaotic Artist**. For genuine model-origin creative comparison, require independent world families and seeds, matched legal host and decoding controls, and uncorrelated original trajectories that are not just STOP truncations. Blind owner A/B/TIE/SKIP across these niche questions must remain absent until supplied; `P20_UNRATED_OWNER_BALLOT.csv` is intentionally blank. Five regenerable A4 pages show **only** the 10 original historical nonidentical pairs (no sources or labels on-sheet).
