## Specific artistic false positive

At full 128-decision horizon, 64-cell Greebles seed 9803 GP010 raises right-angle turning to **35.43%** but visits only **89** distinct cells, versus GP006's **129**, and repeats **29.69%** of traversed edges. This is exactly why a turn-maximising reward risks confusing confined scribbling with interesting Curler/Chaotic-Artist behaviour. P23's source-verified structural screen flags this case. Such a flag is not a human rejection; an owner might still prefer its appearance, which must be recorded separately.
