# QL-019 — Q4 calibration on prospective W0 *surrogate* rollouts

16 November 2026. **Exploratory research / negative behaviour-transfer finding; Draft/HOLD, no promotion.** This is a deliberately bounded, CPU-only experiment. The live scoring policies in this study are fitted **ExtraTrees score emulators**, not the original QL-017 neural policies. New paths are real W0 host rollouts of the emulators, not shadow projections or evidence of original-model on-policy efficacy.

## Validation before interpretation

| Fit to archived policy | Train / held-out stored decisions | Held-out host-masked action agreement | With Q4 added: held-out action agreement | Real-Q4 BRANCH / surrogate-Q4 BRANCH on held-out states |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Branch Blackout / Q1 12%-cap | 863 / 388 | 93.04% | 89.95% | 31 / 28 |
| Branch Mania / Q1 8%-cap | 853 / 387 | 95.61% | 93.80% | 82 / 77 |

The high action agreement does **not** establish equivalent long-horizon trajectories. The score emulators are a fallback necessitated by incomplete availability of the exact frozen QL-003/014/015 checkpoint graph for prospective inference. A few errors can compound over 96 steps. All off-policy validation compares the newly fitted emulator against the exact stored QL-017 logits in previously unseen seed groups; all prospective rollouts use emulator-generated scores only.

## Prospective W0 host results

18 newly played episodes per model/condition (six world seeds × three drawing families). All reported actions are host-legal. **Each episode's world state genuinely changes in response to the selected model action**; no future original-model logits are fabricated.

| Initial personality | Intervention | Coverage, 0% random | BRANCH / RETRACE, 0% | Coverage, 10% random | BRANCH / RETRACE, 10% |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Blackout | Surrogate uncorrected | 35.39 cells | 0 / 139 | **24.00** | 3 / 72 |
| Blackout | Packed Q4 context | **37.17** | 67 / 98 | 22.00 | 39 / 54 |
| Blackout | Equal-mean constant ablation | 35.78 | 66 / 92 | 21.72 | 43 / 53 |
| Mania | Surrogate uncorrected | 35.78 | 274 / 106 | **27.94** | 211 / 69 |
| Mania | Packed Q4 context | 36.22 | 24 / 93 | 27.22 | **17 / 90** |
| Mania | Equal-mean constant ablation | **36.61** | 127 / 95 | 26.44 | 84 / 63 |

No clear general coverage improvement: in the standard 10% host-random cohorts, Q4 reduces coverage from 24.00 to 22.00 cells (Blackout) and from 27.94 to 27.22 cells (Mania). Q4 breaks Blackout and reduces Mania's pathological branching, but in the latter it **over-suppresses** BRANCH far beyond the constant correction (17 vs 84 branches with 10% overrides). The apparent contextual benefit is fragile and depends on both personality and override regime. On clean runs Q4 adds +1.78 cells to Blackout and +0.44 cells to Mania, neither establishing superiority with six world-seed clusters. The companion constant ablation often behaves similarly or better.

There were **216 original PNG trajectories**, **7,674 new legal on-policy W0 decisions** across all cohorts, and **8 independently repeated seed-10747 trajectory/PNG matches**. Archive inventory contains 218 entries. Peak **192.8 MiB shared-process RSS** is not per-model memory. Reference ExtraTrees CPU inference was about 1–2 ms per action; these are *surrogate* costs, not original quantized model throughput. The Q4 correction itself is seven bytes and is only a residual added to a comparatively large forest emulator. Original IQ1/IQ2/IQ3 GGUF codecs remain untested.
