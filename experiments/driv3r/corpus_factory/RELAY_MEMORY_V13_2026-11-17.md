# DRIV3R Corpus Factory v13 — two-event relay memory
17 November 2026 · Existing DRIV3R Draft/HOLD PR #1 · Data-only synthetic-host experiment

## Why
The v8 short-memory policy solved all simple corridors, and v11-v12 supplied a single, relatively easily decoded remote event. V13 requires combining an early relay boot bit, a later relay toggle and a topology-specific wiring orientation to select the open remote corridor. The alternate route is tempting and initially legal but has a shutter near its far end. Side spurs and material textures differ by split.

At the hub, each counterfactual sibling pair has byte-identical current 7×7 material window, position, goal, legal action menu, last64 positions/events and last1 positions, but the **opposite optimal first move**. The old causal event differs. A history-restricted rule cannot exceed 50% on balanced paired first-choice labels; this is a mathematical property, not a measured neural model score.

## Dataset and execution

| **Total** | **416** | **208** | **104** | **3,744** |

27,936 synthetic host-applied oracle moves, all 416 expert episodes arrived, zero protected entries and zero illegal expert moves. Every selected decision target was menu-legal. There are 4,160 legal strictly longer successor-action hard-negative annotations (a subset can occur in many correlated decisions). All 208 counterfactual pairs passed last64 and local alias with opposed labels. World groups and topology grammars do not cross TRAIN/DEV/HELDOUT. Local 7×7 near-duplicate rejection uses Hamming threshold <=4 outside intentional siblings; 0 independent worlds rejected on the default seeds.

Standalone Node 22.16 regression PASS; a second full CLI generation reproduced **all six JSONL SHA-256 digests and the manifest byte-for-byte**. Default corpus generator observed 2.55–2.82 seconds and 138–139 MB peak RSS; regression 5.99 seconds, 193 MB peak RSS. These are generator metrics, not model inference metrics. Exact source/test Git blobs and shard digest/byte-count manifest are in `V13_DEFAULT_MANIFEST.json`.

Store full JSONL shards outside Git; immutable Actions publication is outstanding. **Only row.model_input or createHost/observe(host) belongs in a model prompt**. Never expose row target, expert path, privileged shutter, pairing controls, IDs or provenance as model features. Use TRAIN for optimization, DEV for selection, and inspect sealed HELDOUT only after freezing configuration. Evaluate by world group, with last64, last1 and zero-history controls and exact host-legal action enforcement.

## Limits and next consumer
This is not native CyberSand; material patterns are visual tokens, not physical material flow. The >200-position historical prelude is scripted rather than generated by the model, and the binary relay mechanism remains symbolically simple. It establishes data integrity and repeatable oracle replay, **not model competence**. Paint/motif training remains in v11. No model weights, adapter or native ABI were changed/qualified.

Read-only consumer context: the LFM lane reports an actual pinned GP350-006 model adapter but branch/retrace remains 0 in its cited matched episodes; the Sprocket lane is blocked on its pinned root files/dependencies. The next experiment belongs to a trainer lane: pinned actual SLM full-history versus last64/last1 on TRAIN/DEV, then fully model-controlled sealed HELDOUT with arrival, violations, path stretch, p50/p95 latency and peak RSS. No writes to CyberSand or Neurounner.
